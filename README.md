### Project Description
FAAA TUBE is a delightfully annoying video player where every YouTube video plays for just 2 seconds and immediately freezes. The only way to squeeze out a few more frames is to chase and click a tiny red sports car driving wildly all over your screen while it screams "FAAAA" on every tap. Once you reach 28 clicks, the car engine completely blows up and throws you into a scammy subscription paywall—one where you can't actually buy anything, and you can never exit.

### The Problem (that doesn't exist)
Watching videos online is way too easy and relaxing. People just sit on their couches, stare at the screen, and never have to sweat, panic, or work hard for their entertainment.

### The Solution (that nobody asked for)
We built a player that makes watching a 10-second meme feel like a full-body workout. Playback stops dead in its tracks at 00:02, forcing you to manually hunt down a roaming red car to crank frames forward. Every 7 clicks, the car drifts across the screen to make you miss. Once you burn through 28 clicks, the engine dies and traps you in a subscription screen. If you try to close it, the "X" button dodges your mouse; if you try to pay, it insults you and rejects your card anyway.
